# SlashBuilder

**The open-source organization at the root of household computing.**

SlashBuilder stewards the open protocol and open operating system that
Lockamy Studios' products are built on. It sells nothing. What it publishes,
anyone can build on — including us.

---

## What we build

SlashBuilder stewards two peer projects. Neither is subordinate to the
other — the same shape as the Apache Software Foundation hosting Kafka and
Spark side by side.

### Hearth

The open protocol for distributed personal resources. 9P-inspired — the
same lineage as Plan 9, reapplied to a household rather than a data center.
Hearth is how devices, services, and the people who use them address one
another: capability-grants instead of accounts, a thin wire contract
instead of a platform.

### BenixOS

The operating system built around Hearth. A spiritual successor to two
lineages at once: **Plan 9** (the Bell Labs Unix successor — "Unix 2.0,"
built by the original Unix developers) for its distributed-systems bones,
and **BeOS** for its Kit-model architecture and design sensibility. BenixOS
runs on Thunderhead-class hardware — the general term for a home-server
appliance, the way "NAS" or "router" describe a category rather than a
product.

---

## Why an open organization, not a product

Publishing the protocol and the OS separately from any commercial product
means the household layer survives independent of any one company's
fortunes. Apache-2.0 on the code, CC-BY-4.0 on the specs. Fork it, run it,
build a business on it — SlashBuilder doesn't gate any of that behind a
license key or an account.

**[Quickring](https://quickring.me)** is the commercial packager built on
top of what SlashBuilder ships — software, services, and hardware, sold
under its own name. SlashBuilder doesn't sell hardware, doesn't sell
software, and doesn't operate a hosted service. It publishes.
